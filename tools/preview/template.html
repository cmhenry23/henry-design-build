    Before this goes live

    Three things need real information

    Testimonials are invented

    The five quotes are examples written to show the layout — not real reviews. Publishing invented reviews misleads customers and is prohibited under Canada's Competition Act. Replace them in data/testimonials.ts, or empty the array and the section hides itself.

    Budget rates are guesses

    Seeded from published 2026 Ontario guides, not Ryan's jobs. Divide a few finished invoices by square footage, put those into BUILD_TYPES, and check the tool against a project already completed.

    Project facts need confirming

    Every project name, location, year and duration was inferred from the photos. Email, phone and service area are TODO in data/site.ts. The About page also needs a real portrait of Ryan.
